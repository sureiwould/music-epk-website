About Me

I'm Kevin Sclar, a Jacksonville-based guitarist and multi-instrumentalist with a lifelong passion for performing, recording, and collaborating with other musicians.

From my early days performing in California to recording in Nashville and becoming part of Jacksonville's music scene, music has always been at the center of my life.

Musical Background

My passion for music began at an early age, largely thanks to my father, who was also a multi-instrumentalist. Growing up surrounded by music, I developed a love for playing and creating it that has stayed with me ever since.

I began playing keyboard at just three years old before picking up the guitar at six. As my musical interests continued to grow, I expanded to bass guitar at sixteen and drums at eighteen. Learning multiple instruments has given me a broader understanding of music and how each instrument contributes to the overall sound.

Performance Experience

I grew up in California, where I began performing at an early age. From childhood talent shows to playing in bands as a teenager, I took every opportunity I could to get onstage. Whether it was a bar, a backyard, or just about anywhere with room to perform, I was always looking for the next chance to play.

I later moved to Nashville, Tennessee, where I continued refining my craft and gained a deeper understanding of what it takes to be a professional musician. One of my most memorable experiences was having the opportunity to record and perform with Lee Offman, the original creator of the Miami Dolphins theme song. During our time working together, I recorded guitar for a version of the song featured in an NFL documentary about him. It was an experience that helped shape my development as a musician and remains one of the highlights of my musical journey.

In 2023, I moved to Jacksonville, Florida, and immediately began looking for ways to get involved in the local music scene. Ended up joining a cover band shortly thereafter, and the first show I played in Jax was at the Underbelly! I started attending shows, networking, and meeting other musicians, eventually finding a great community at Kava & Co.

Since then, I've been involved in a variety of musical projects, including Kevin and Friends, Dead Letter Service, Redbeard and Friends, and Shower Beer. I've also had the opportunity to accompany local singer-songwriters for live performances at venues throughout Jacksonville.

One of my recent highlights was winning the 3rd Annual Kava & Co. Battle of the Bands with Shower Beer. It was an especially meaningful experience considering how important Kava & Co. had been in helping me connect with Jacksonville's music community.

From my early days performing in California to the experiences I gained in Nashville and the musical community I've found in Jacksonville, music has remained a constant part of my life. I continue to look for opportunities to perform, collaborate, and grow as a musician.
